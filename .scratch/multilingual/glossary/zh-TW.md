# zh-TW glossary (Traditional Chinese, Taiwan)

Bible tradition: Chinese Union Version (和合本), **神版**: "God" is always 神 (never 上帝). Address to the user: 你. Address to God in prayers: 祢 (common Taiwanese church usage, mirrors the capitalised "You").
Spacing: one half-width space between CJK and Latin letters or numbers (Apple/Taiwan app convention); full-width punctuation in Chinese text.
