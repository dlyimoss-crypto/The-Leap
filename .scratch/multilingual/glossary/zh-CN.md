# zh-CN glossary (Simplified Chinese)

Tradition: Chinese Union Version 和合本, **神版** (the edition most mainland Protestant churches use). "God" is always 神, never 上帝. Address form: 你 (also 你 when addressing God in prayer, as is common in mainland churches; 祢 is avoided). Titles follow the name: Pastor Deniss → Deniss 牧师. A half-width space separates Chinese from Latin words and numerals (The Leap, PD, AI, `{count}`).
